## Spanish Student Learning Outcomes

| Outcomes | Sub outcomes |
| :---- | :---- |
| SLO 1: **Language Proficiency** *Upon completion of the Spanish major, students will be able to  **communicate effectively in Spanish in a variety of contexts according to ACTFL criteria for Advanced Low level.*** | (A) **(Interpretive communication) …**understand the main message and supporting details on a wide variety of topics across various time frames from complex, organized texts that are spoken and written. |
|  | (B) **(Interpersonal communication)** …maintain spontaneous conversations and discussions across various time frames on familiar and unfamiliar, concrete topics using a series of connecting sentences and probing questions. |
|  | (C) **(Presentational communication)** …produce detailed and organized presentations on familiar as well as unfamiliar concrete topics, in paragraphs and using various time frames through spoken and written language. |
| SLO 2: **Cultural Analysis** *Upon completion of the Spanish major, students will be able to*  ***analyze and contextualize a variety of cultural productions of Spanish-speaking cultures and communities.*** | (A) (**Analyze)** analyze the form and content of literary, artistic, and other cultural productions |
|  | (B) **(Contextualize)** …  interpret cultural productions and the perspectives they represent by applying knowledge of historical, geographical, political, and social contexts  |
|  | (C) **(Reflect)** …reflect on their experiences with products in Spanish-speaking cultures to recognize their cultural assumptions and suspend judgment |
| SLO 3: **Language Analysis** *Upon completion of the Spanish major students will be able to  **analyze and contextualize language variation across the Spanish-speaking world.*** | (A) (**Analyze)** …analyze language samples by applying knowledge of linguistic systems and language acquisition. |
|  | (B) **(Contextualize)** …apply knowledge of language variation to identify differences of dialect, sociolect, and register across time, regions, cultures and communities, including historically marginalized populations. |
|  | (C) **(Reflect)** …reflect on their experiences using Spanish to interact with people from Spanish-speaking communities to evaluate progress and identify areas for improvement |
