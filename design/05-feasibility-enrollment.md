# SPA 379 — Feasibility & Enrollment (recruit-as-anchor)

> The seminar is designed as an **anchor** for a restructured linguistics track, recruited **broadly** (any motivated post-320). Broad recruitment is the biggest unvalidated assumption. This is the cheap, pre-commitment check. **Action owner: Jerid** (poll the actual student body; not executable by the plan itself).

## Why validate

Spanish-medium + a critical-linguist disposition + "typology" label is a hard sell to a broad cohort. If recruitment collapses to a few strong self-selectors, we pay the immersion tax **without** the anchor payoff. Confirming demand is cheaper than redesigning in Spring 2027.

## Framing (the ask students say yes to)

Don't sell "tipología." Sell the question:

> **¿Es tu español un dato — o la norma?**
> Un seminario en español sobre *por qué* hablan distinto en los países hispanohablantes — y cómo se decide si es "otro tipo de español" o solo "más o menos frecuente".

Catalog blurb (≈ 3 lines, question-led, not label-led):
> *SPA 379 — ¿Hay un solo español? Análisis de la variación del español con datos reales. ¿Por qué el Caribe usa más pronombres? ¿Se "pierde" el subjuntivo? Pensar como lingüista crítico, en español. Prereq: SPA 320.*

## Interest poll (5–7 min; run in SPA 320 sections + linguistics minor list)

1. Which questions sound interesting? (multi) — why Spanish varies by region · whether there's a "correct" Spanish · how languages influence each other · how to back a claim with data · none.
2. Course taught **entirely in Spanish** (discussion + writing): interested / with reservations / not for me. *(tests the immersion assumption directly.)*
3. Confidence using terms like *subjunctive/pro-drop/dialect*: low / med / high. *(baseline diagnostic; informs the glosario + scaffolds.)*
4. Would you take a seminar that uses **data apps** (interpret real distributions) rather than only readings? yes / no.
5. Hours of weekly reading you'd accept: <5 / 5–8 / 8+ *(calibrate the ~40–60 pg budget).*
6. Free: what would make you pick this over another seminar?

## Feasibility gate (decide before Phase 5 content-lock)

| If … | Then |
| :-- | :-- |
| ≥ ~8 of a realistic ~10–15 pool "interested," incl. some post-320 with reservations | Proceed broad + supports as designed. |
| Strong-but-narrow interest (mostly high-confidence students) | Keep the arc; **shift marketing** to the syntax/grad-bound niche; trim to fewer supports. |
| Weak interest in Spanish-medium | Reconsider: bilingual content + Spanish *output* only (revisit D-instruction), or reposition catalog framing. |
