# SPA 379 — Stage 2: Acceptable Evidence (Backwards Design)

> Status: Stage 2 of UbD. Depends on LOCKED Stage 1 (`01-stage1-desired-results.md`). Determines **what counts as proof** that students reached the transfer goal and the SLO-mapped outcomes (3.2 core; 1.1, 1.3 mapped; 3.1.4 pooled) — *before* Stage 3 sequences activities.
> Method note (W&McT, two-dimensional task grid): each task is placed in a **category** (A–E below) *and* anchored to a **goal** (transfer + SLO). This makes the whole set defensible and exposes gaps.

---

## The transfer goal, as evidence

**Transfer:** *act as a critical linguist about Spanish, in Spanish* — observe real variation → explain it with a testable linguistic account (not a verdict) → interrogate the categories (language/dialect/type/norm/correct) → reflect on one's own position.

A disposition is **not** provable by one exam. Evidence therefore splits:
- **Ongoing, low-stakes reasoning** (the evidence log) — repeated practice of the transfer moves across every unit.
- **Authentic summative performances** (contextualized analysis, capstone defense) — the move done at full weight.
- **Open-question handling** — tasks with no single right answer where students weigh evidence, mirroring the thesis.

Six facets of understanding mapped: Explain (analyses), Interpret (reading + data), Apply (parameter scoring), Perspective (two markedness scales), Empathy/Positionality (own variety as data), Knowledge (toolkit).

## Rubrics (Spanish, keyed to SLO criteria — step 6)

> Department instruments use **binary criterion questions** (¿Sí/No?) per sub-outcome (see `documents/spa-dept-learning-outcomes.md`). Delivery rubrics mirror those criteria **and** add a 3-level quality band (En desarrollo / Competente / Sólido) so the seminar reports both the department Y/N and a holistic judgment. All in **Spanish** (student-facing).

### R-T2 · Análisis contextualizado (SLO 3.2)

**Criterios binarios (reportables):**
- **3.2.1** ¿Usa terminología lingüística para contrastar variedades del español (o español y otra lengua)? S/N
- **3.2.2** ¿Conecta la producción lingüística con al menos una característica del hablante (región, clase, edad, contacto)? S/N
- **3.2.3** ¿Conecta la forma con una influencia histórica/social/lingüística — p. ej., contacto, política lingüística, o un **proceso tipológicamente frecuente**? S/N

**Banda de calidad:**
- *Sólido:* distingue las **dos escalas de marcados** (tipológica vs. interna) sin confundirlas; interpreta los datos reales (no solo describe); juzga tipo-vs-frecuencia con criterio explícito; menciona una limitación de los datos (muestreo/comparabilidad).
- *Competente:* cumple los tres criterios pero la interpretación es parcial; una escala de marcados se aplica de forma inconsistente.
- *En desarrollo:* describe sin contextualizar; confunde variación con error; ignora el problema del muestreo.

### R-T3 · Defensa interpretativa (SLO 1.3, +3.1.4)

**Criterios binarios:**
- **1.3.4** ¿Plantea una tesis o postura clara? S/N
- **1.3.5** ¿Ofrece evidencia de apoyo (datos, estudios, ejemplos)? S/N
- **1.3.6** ¿Conecta ideas y guía al lector/audiencia con conectores? S/N

**Banda de calidad (disposición crítica):**
- *Sólido:* la postura es **defendible y matizada**; reconoce el desacuerdo experto y el peso de la evidencia (no presenta una hipótesis como hecho); **cierra con positionality** (su propia variedad como dato, un supuesto que traía); maneja la pregunta abierta.
- *Competente:* tesis + evidencia presentes pero el manejo de contra-evidencia o del carácter abierto es débil.
- *En desarrollo:* opinión sin evidencia; verdicto prescriptivo sin cuenta del carácter abierto de la pregunta.

### R-T4 · Lectura interpretativa (SLO 1.1)

- **1.1.4** ¿Reformula la tesis principal del texto informativo? S/N
- **1.1.5** ¿Identifica subargumentos o piezas de evidencia de apoyo? S/N
- *Supports note:* glossary access permitted (broad+supports); the criterion is comprehension, not vocabulary recall.

### R-T5 · Descriptivo vs. prescriptivo (SLO 3.1.4) — *depth-ceiling*

- **3.1.4** ¿Diferencia enunciados descriptivos y prescriptivos sobre el español? S/N
- Plus: rewrites one prescriptive claim into a **descriptive account** (rule-governed variation), correctly.
- *Why here:* this is the pooled contribution that proves the seminar does **analysis at depth** (SLO 3.1.4), not just contextualizing — the README's "never do primary-data analysis" gap, closed.

### R-T6 · GenAI: ¿un dato o la norma? (SLO 3.2.2–3, 1.1.5)

- ¿Trata la salida del modelo como **agregado** (sobre su corpus de entrenamiento), no como "el español"? S/N
- ¿Identifica al menos una **variiedad/dialecto/registro** que el modelo aplanó o estereotipó, y la contrasta con evidencia real? S/N
- ¿Vincula esa distorsión con el **problema del muestreo / comparabilidad**? S/N
- *Rubric-light:* guided; pass/fail on the three moves + one concrete example. Non-delegable core: **student interprets the real data before** consulting the model.

## Grading architecture (step 8)

Philosophy: **the disposition is the point, so the grade rewards repeated reasoning, not recall.** Weight the low-stakes, frequent evidence log and the authentic defenses; keep readings/quiz-like items low-stakes. All student-facing descriptions in Spanish.

| Component | Task(s) | Weight | Type | Rationale |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Cuaderno de evidencia** | T1 | **20%** | formative (best-N counted) | Makes the transfer move habitual; frequent, low-stakes. |
| **Análisis contextualizados** | T2 (×2) | **30%** | summative | Core SLO 3.2 evidence; data-driven. |
| **Defensa interpretativa (capstone)** | T3 | **30%** | summative | SLO 1.3 + open-question handling; oral defense in final block. |
| **Actividad GenAI + ítem descriptivo/prescriptivo** | T6 + T5 | **10%** | summative (guided) | Disposition (descriptive stance) + critical AI literacy. |
| **Lectura + participación** | T4 + discussion | **10%** | formative | SLO 1.1 + scaffolded-talk supports; rewards the *expanded* participation model. |

- **Late work / attendance / accessibility:** standard WFU patterns → to be finalized in syllabus (Phase 4).
- **Proficiency floor (SLO 1.3):** the capstone defense must reach the department's 1.3.4–6 Y/N to pass; scaffolds (phrase cards, drafts, optional T6) are the broad+supports mitigation, not lower standards.

## D6 — GenAI strand, placement (step 10, DECIDED)

**Form: one discrete activity (T6), not a recurring theme.** (A daily "warm-up" framing was rejected in review.)

- **Placement:** Unit C (contact typology + language/dialect ideology) — the natural context, because contact/ideology is where "whose Spanish is the norm?" is live. ~1 session.
- **Core move:** GenAI output = **aggregate over a (skewed) training corpus**. Students treat the model as a *sample*, interrogate whether it represents real Spanish use (whole or in part), and tie the distortion to the **sampling / comparability problem (U5)** — making the abstract method constraint concrete and device-native.
- **Optional tooling use (AI-as-tooling, D7):** students may use GenAI to help build/communicate an analysis (Tier-2/3), mirroring how the instructor builds tooling (D4). **Not graded toward an outcome**; transferable liberal-arts skill only.
- **Policy (feeds syllabus §V, D7):** three AI roles named; **non-delegable core** for T6 = *interpret the real corpus before consulting the model*; per-task permitted/encouraged/prohibited language (not "AI allowed/prohibited").
