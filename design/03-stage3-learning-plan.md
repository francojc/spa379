## Step 11 — Part I budget (LOCKED, lean)

**6 sessions (S01–S06), no more.** The README's "mini-methods course" risk is contained by three rules:
1. **Method taught through ONE worked micro-example** (S04: a single WALS/Grambank feature, end to end) — not an abstract methods sequence.
2. **No world-survey** — typology is a *lens*, not a tour; every toolkit item exists to be re-invoked in Part II.
3. **Module 0 (4 sessions) → Module 1 (2 sessions)**, and Module 1 *only* establishes the pan-Hispanic baseline + the two-markedness scales + Spanish's marked profile (the setup for A and B). The databases' provenance (the "how the sausage is made") is the S04 micro-example — this pre-empts the black-box critique at its root.

## Step 12 — Arc & sequencing (LOCKED)

- **A+B deep (7 each), C synthesis (5), capstone (3 + final block), Part I (6).** 6 + 7 + 7 + 5 + 3 = **28** ✓.
- **Language-vs-dialect frame** threaded (Abstand/Ausbau; S05 baseline, S24 explicit) rather than siloed — the anti-"two-halves" mechanism.
- **Two-markedness thread** re-invoked at every unit open (the 30-second toolkit rappel in S07/S14/S21) and is the spine question each deep unit closes on.
- **Convergence-to-universal** carried as a *question-to-test* (S20 bridge, S25 synthesis), never a thesis-to-defend.

## Step 16 — Supports architecture (LOCKED)

The mitigation for **Spanish-medium × broad cohort** — mandatory, not optional (else the course collapses to a few strong students).

- **Glosario bilingüe de términos clave** (tipología/marcado/pro-drop/subjuntivo/Abstand-Ausbau…) — Spanish definitions, English gloss; living page, seeded in Part I, grows each unit.
- **Banco de lectura Spanish-first** (from D3) with **short Spanish framing intros** so the L2 academic register is scaffolded, not dumped.
- **Scaffolded-talk phrase cards** — Spanish moves for the disposition: "Esto es **descriptivo, no prescriptivo** porque…" · "¿Qué **evidencia** resolvería esto?" · "Cuidado: aquí hay un **sesgo de muestreo**…" · "Mi propio español assume que…" (used in every Taller/plenaria; also the T3 positionality closer).
- **Tiered tasks** (T1/T2/T3) so depth is opt-in without lowering the floor.
- **Expanded participation** credit (listening, clarifying questions, reflective posts) so talk-style doesn't gate grading.
- **Draft + feedback loop** for the capstone (S26–S27) before the oral defense.
